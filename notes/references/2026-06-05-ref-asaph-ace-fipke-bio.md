# Asaph "Ace" Fipke

## Personal

- **Born:** 1970, British Columbia. Twin sister Leila; siblings Johanna and Beau.
- **Father:** Wayne Clifford Fipke (1948–2022) — General Manager of Citadel Theatre in Edmonton (1977–1988), MFA from UBC, co-founded Edmonton International Children's Festival. Later left theatre and helped his brother Chuck bring DiaMet public.
- **Uncle:** Charles "Chuck" Fipke — Canadian geologist who discovered diamonds at Lac de Gras, NWT (1991). Wayne was Chuck's brother and business partner on the DiaMet IPO — one of the biggest stories in Canadian mining history.
- **Mother:** Ava Fipke
- **Spouse:** Hemsa Fipke
- **Children:** Two sons, Zane and Elias
- **Residence:** West Vancouver — the Eppich House, designed by Arthur Erickson (1972)
- **Education:** UBC liberal arts (incomplete), Vancouver Film School (film production, 1990s)

## Career — Animation (mid-1990s–2017)

### Mainframe Entertainment
- Entry-level production work in CGI animation
- Executive producer on *ReBoot* (1994–2001)
- Associate producer, *Beast Wars: Transformers* S2 (1997)
- Producer, *Beast Machines: Transformers* (1999–2001)
- Senior VP of development and production (by 2000)

### Nerd Corps Entertainment (2002–2014)
- **Co-founder and CEO** (with Chuck Johnson, fellow Mainframe producer)
- Grew from 10 to ~420 employees
- Created/wrote/directed *Storm Hawks* (2007–2009, 52 episodes)
- Co-created *The League of Super Evil* (2009–2012, 52 eps; Leo Award 2011)
- Executive producer, *Slugterra* (2012–2016, 63 eps + 6 films; Daytime Emmy 2014)
- Also produced: *Dragon Booster* (Gemini 2005), *Hot Wheels: Battle Force 5*, *Max Steel*, *Kate & Mim-Mim*, *Monster High* shorts
- Voiced Radarr in all 52 *Storm Hawks* episodes
- Produced 300+ episodes broadcast in 150+ territories

### DHX Media / WildBrain (2014–2017)
- Nerd Corps acquired for **CAD $57 million** (December 2014)
- Chief Content Officer, Kids & Family division
- Oversaw DHX Studios Vancouver (57,000 sq ft)
- Managed *Mega Man* series (co-developed with Dentsu, premiered 2018)
- Departed 2017

## Career — Environmental Ventures (2017–present)

- Founded **Earth Story Inc.**, now operating as **Earth Invest Inc.**
- CEO — carbon offset funding, REDD+ projects, afforestation in sub-Saharan Africa
- Integrates media/documentary tools for climate awareness
- Aligns with UN's 17 Sustainable Development Goals
